  - type: "cards"
    items:
      - eyebrow: "Build"
        title: "Development"
        body: "The app was built by translating wireframes into reusable SwiftUI views backed by a structured local data model. Clothing items, outfits, and canvas elements were modeled as separate but connected entities, allowing items to be reused across multiple outfits while preserving individual position, scale, and rotation on the outfit canvas. A flexible tagging system was also implemented to support custom \"tags\" that can be applied to both items and outfits, enabling more personalized organization and filtering."
      - eyebrow: "Reflection"
        title: "Challenges & Learnings"
        body: "Working with user-generated images introduced layout challenges related to varying aspect ratios and scaling within fixed UI components. Building the outfit canvas also required separating visual placement data from item data, reinforcing the importance of data modeling when designing interactive, state-driven interfaces."
